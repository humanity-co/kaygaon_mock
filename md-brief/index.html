What we need

The mill yard and weighbridge at sunrise

The problem today

Bills wait 2–3 days for paper to travel.

Papers travel by hand

A truck arrives and QC happens during unloading. The GRN (Goods Receipt Note) and supporting papers are carried to the corporate office. This takes about 2–3 days.

Corporate waits

Accounts can't check or post the bill until the papers arrive.

Phone calls and paper

Status is tracked by calls and paper. Documents can go missing.

No live view

Management has no single live view of trucks, bills, stock, production or money.

Our idea in one line

We work around DataMan, not instead of it.

A simple factory app plus a web dashboard. DataMan stays exactly as it is and remains the official accounting book. We read its Excel reports. We never change or write into DataMan.

  • DataMan ERP Unchanged, still the official booksStays the same
  • Bill posting Accounts keeps posting bills in DataMan exactly as todayStays the same
  • Original papers Kept wherever law or audit needs themStays the same
  • Approvals No automatic approval of payments or deductions. People decide.Stays the same

How a truck will flow

Six steps, from gate to posted bill.

01

Gate

One digital entry per truck

Security creates the entry and photographs the papers.

02

Weighbridge

Gross and tare weights recorded

Ideally read straight from the weighbridge, not typed.

03

QC during unloading

Result recorded on a phone, with photos

  • Accepted
  • Partly accepted
  • On hold
  • Rejected

04

GRN

One digital document packet

The official GRN number is linked. The packet holds the GRN, invoice, weighbridge slip and QC report.

05

Corporate

Corporate sees it the same day

Accounts can start checking immediately. The original papers still travel, but are tracked with a handover and receipt.

06

Bill check and posting

Invoice matched against PO and GRN

Mismatches go to the right person. Accounts post in DataMan as usual, and the dashboard confirms it from DataMan's own report.
